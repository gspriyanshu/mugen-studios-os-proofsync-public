30 / 60 / 90

Move from eligibility to evidence.

Days 0–30

Freeze baseline; repair crawl/index controls; map intent and entities; publish priority pages; establish Search Console, analytics, conversion, and change annotations.

Days 31–60

Expand high-intent coverage; strengthen internal links; ship answer modules; improve image delivery; begin authority outreach; compare holdout and changed page groups.

Days 61–90

Review discovery, non-brand visibility, qualified conversions, field performance, earned citations, and available generative-AI data; keep, revise, or stop each intervention.

KPI contract

Report the layer where evidence exists.

  • Crawl errors and valid canonical coverage
  • Indexed pages and discovery lag
  • Non-brand impressions, clicks, CTR, and position
  • Priority-query and landing-page cohorts
  • Qualified organic conversions and assisted revenue
  • LCP, INP, and CLS field distributions
  • Content freshness and expert-review completion
  • Earned referring domains, mentions, and source quality
  • Dedicated Google Generative AI impressions where available
  • Bing citations, cited pages, and sampled grounding queries

Use Google’s dedicated Generative AI performance report where available; otherwise use overall Search Console performance. Bing’s public-preview AI Performance data can show citations, cited pages, and sampled grounding queries. Citation counts do not mean ranking, authority, or guaranteed placement.

Authority and digital PR

Earn references by making evidence worth citing.

Create source value

Publish original decisions, methods, data, expert commentary, tools, templates, and clearly updated facts.

Match real audiences

Map journalists, communities, partners, directories, and editorial pages to the evidence they genuinely need.

Audit quality

Track relevance, editorial independence, link/mention context, referral value, and downstream discovery. Do not buy authority or manufacture mentions.

Holdout, edge, and claims boundary

Stress the method where mistakes cost more.

Holdout · mental health

A page fails if it lacks credentialed review, traceable sources, current guidance, user-first explanation, visible limitations, or responsible reporting. Search optimization never overrides clinical accuracy or safety.

Edge · B2B fintech

A program fails if automation scales ambiguous entities, stale facts, unreviewed schema, or content without conversion and revenue hypotheses. Human review and experiment stop rules remain mandatory.

This demonstration reports technical and editorial readiness. Outcome evidence begins only after search engines discover and measure an approved route. Any displayed score is an internal evaluation, not proof of rankings or commercial results.

Current primary guidance

Measurement and markup follow the platforms’ published rules.

  • Google: Optimizing for generative AI — useful, accessible, people-first content; no special AI-only markup requirement.
  • Google: Generative AI performance reports — dedicated views launched to a subset of sites in June 2026.
  • Google: structured data guidelines — markup must represent visible content and does not guarantee a search feature.
  • Bing: AI Performance public preview — citations, cited pages, sampled grounding queries, and explicit interpretation limits.
