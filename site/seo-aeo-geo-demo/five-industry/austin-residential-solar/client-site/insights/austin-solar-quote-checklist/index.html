The short answer

How can homeowners make solar quotes comparable?

Normalize equipment models, system size, production assumptions, shade, price basis, financing fees, warranties, workmanship, monitoring, roof work, permit and interconnection steps, service responsibility and every stated incentive.

What this cannot decide

The checklist does not endorse an installer or create a price benchmark.

01

Normalise scope before ranking price

Put every proposal into the same table: DC and AC size, panel and inverter models, storage capacity, racking, electrical upgrades, roof work, monitoring, permits and utility tasks. A missing line item should be marked excluded or unresolved, not assumed to be included.

Compare the cash price before financing so dealer fees do not masquerade as equipment value. Then add financing terms, incentives and optional work as separate layers.

02

Interrogate the production model

Each quote should disclose annual production, modelling software or method, weather source, shade loss, orientation, degradation and availability assumptions. A larger output estimate is not automatically a better design when the assumptions differ.

Ask for a layout and a reproducible path from roof planes to the annual estimate. If a performance guarantee exists, read the exclusions, measurement source and remedy rather than relying on the headline percentage.

03

Map responsibility through the system life

The proposal should name the seller, installer, electrical contractor, permit applicant, utility-program participant, equipment warrantors, monitoring provider and service contact. These may be different organisations, and each responsibility should survive the sales conversation in writing.

Verify applicable registrations, utility participation and actual individual credentials in their primary directories. A logo on a proposal is not enough, and directory inclusion should not be described as government or utility endorsement.
