Skincare Claim Evidence Matrix

A source-led matrix for deciding what proof must exist before common skincare and packaging claims go live in India.

Publisher: MUGEN Studios OSPublished and reviewed 17 July 2026Specialist review: pending for real-client use

How to use it

Work through the rows before a real client publishes, submits or promises anything. Every decision needs a source or client record, an accountable owner, a checked date and a visible limitation.

Truth boundary

This is an indexable MUGEN methodology resource. It does not represent an operating responsible skincare ecommerce business or a measured client outcome.
