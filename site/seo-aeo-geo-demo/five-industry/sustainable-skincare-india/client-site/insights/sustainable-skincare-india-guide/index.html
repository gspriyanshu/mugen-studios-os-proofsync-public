The short answer

How should a buyer assess a sustainable skincare claim?

Break the claim into formula, sourcing, packaging, operations, certification and disposal. Ask for evidence at each layer, confirm the exact product and geography, and treat anything unverified as an aspiration rather than a result.

What this cannot decide

The fictional range has no real formulas, packaging or certification to evaluate.

Start with the decision, not the category label

The query “sustainable skincare India” is useful only when it helps a reader make the decision behind it. In India, that means separating the general rule from the property, product, programme, professional or operating fact that a real client would still need to verify.

This sequence lens keeps the answer specific: begin with the decision, attach the closest responsible source, record what remains unknown and link the reader to the page that owns the next step. The limitation above is part of the answer, not a disclaimer added after it.
