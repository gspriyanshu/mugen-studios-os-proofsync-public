Test the claim before trusting the conclusion

The query “sustainable beauty claims evidence” is useful only when it helps a reader make the decision behind it. In India, that means separating the general rule from the property, product, programme, professional or operating fact that a real client would still need to verify.

This claim lens keeps the answer specific: begin with the decision, attach the closest responsible source, record what remains unknown and link the reader to the page that owns the next step. The limitation above is part of the answer, not a disclaimer added after it.
