The short answer

What questions expose a weak sustainability claim?

Ask what exactly is claimed, which product or component it covers, the evidence owner, geography, verification source, date, operating boundary, measured result, exclusion, reviewer and next recheck. If those fields are absent, narrow the claim.

What this cannot decide

The checklist does not certify or endorse a product.

Turn the search question into a reusable record

The query “eco friendly skincare claims” is useful only when it helps a reader make the decision behind it. In India, that means separating the general rule from the property, product, programme, professional or operating fact that a real client would still need to verify.

This record lens keeps the answer specific: begin with the decision, attach the closest responsible source, record what remains unknown and link the reader to the page that owns the next step. The limitation above is part of the answer, not a disclaimer added after it.
