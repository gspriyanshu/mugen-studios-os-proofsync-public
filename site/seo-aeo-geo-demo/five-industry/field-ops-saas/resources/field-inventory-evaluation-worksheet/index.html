Field Inventory Evaluation Worksheet

A criteria-first worksheet for testing whether a field-service platform can control parts across dispatch, vans, branches and jobs.

How to use it

Work through the rows before a real client publishes, submits or promises anything. Every decision needs a source or client record, an accountable owner, a checked date and a visible limitation.

Truth boundary

This is an indexable MUGEN methodology resource. It does not represent an operating field-service and inventory saas business or a measured client outcome.
