The short answer

What does field service inventory management control?

It controls how service parts move through branches, vans and work orders, who owns each event, and how the operating record reaches replenishment and accounting without losing the reason behind a change.

What this cannot decide

This guide describes an operating model, not the verified capability of the fictional product.

01

Inventory is a chain of custody, not a stock total

Field-service inventory begins before a technician opens a van. A service request implies a job, the job implies likely parts, and each part must remain attached to a location and status as it moves from branch stock to reservation, issue and use.

A single quantity-on-hand field cannot explain whether a part is free, promised to tomorrow's work, already loaded on a truck or awaiting a return decision. The operating model therefore needs both physical locations and event states, with a named role accountable for every transition.

02

Connect dispatch decisions to part decisions

A dispatcher can assign the right technician and still create a failed visit if the required part exists only at another branch. Before a job is released, the workflow should expose required skills, required parts, source location and any unresolved shortage on the same decision surface.

Reservation is useful because it makes future demand visible without pretending the part has been consumed. If the schedule changes, the reservation needs an explicit release or reassignment event so the stock does not remain unavailable for an abandoned job.

03

Close the loop after the work

Job completion should record what was installed, what came back unused, what was damaged and what remains in the technician's custody. Those facts support replenishment and audit; a generic completed status does not.

Exceptions deserve their own queue. Negative stock, substitutions, missing serial numbers and unprocessed returns should be reviewable by branch and technician, with timestamps and corrective notes rather than silent balance edits.
