The short answer

What is the difference between open and employer-specific work permits?

An employer-specific permit generally authorizes work under named conditions; an open work permit is available only in certain circumstances and may allow work for most compliant employers. The correct category depends on current rules and individual facts.

What this cannot decide

This page cannot determine permit eligibility or advise which application to make.

01

Read the conditions on the actual permit

IRCC distinguishes employer-specific and open work permits, but the label is only the start. An employer-specific permit generally contains conditions tied to the named employment, while an open permit is available only in defined situations and may still contain restrictions. The document and current IRCC instructions control the general description.

A comparison article should direct a permit holder to read the employer, occupation, location, validity and any other printed conditions rather than infer freedom to work from a web summary. It cannot decide whether a job, employer or activity complies with those conditions.

02

Do not treat open permits as a universal option

IRCC's work-permit tool says open permits are available in special circumstances, whereas employer-specific applications commonly involve a job offer and may involve an LMIA or an LMIA-exempt route. A reader should use the official questionnaire and instructions for the category connected to their situation.

The article must avoid suggesting that one permit type is better or easier. Eligibility, fees, evidence and employer steps depend on the route. General content can compare how restrictions are expressed, but it cannot select the permit or confirm that an exemption applies.

03

Treat a job change as a new compliance question

IRCC publishes separate instructions for changing jobs or employers. Its page explains that an employer-specific permit holder generally needs to apply for a new permit if eligible, while an open-permit holder can change employers while the permit remains valid, subject to the permit's conditions and compliant-employer rules.

Before any change, record the current permit conditions, proposed employer and role, relevant application status and the official page consulted. This is a preparation record only. The simulation cannot authorize work, interpret maintained status or tell a person when they may begin a new job.
