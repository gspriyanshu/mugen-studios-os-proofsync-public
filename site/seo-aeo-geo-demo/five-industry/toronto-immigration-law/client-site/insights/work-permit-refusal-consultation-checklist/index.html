The short answer

What records should someone gather after a work-permit refusal?

Keep the refusal letter, complete application and forms, every uploaded document, payment and submission confirmations, correspondence, current status information and a dated timeline. Share them securely with an authorized professional for individual advice.

What this cannot decide

The checklist does not diagnose the refusal, calculate time limits or choose a remedy.

A confident answer still needs a boundary

The query “work permit refusal checklist Canada” is useful only when it helps a reader make the decision behind it. In Toronto, Ontario, that means separating the general rule from the property, product, programme, professional or operating fact that a real client would still need to verify.
