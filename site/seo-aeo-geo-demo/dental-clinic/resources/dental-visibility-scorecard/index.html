Dental Visibility Readiness Scorecard

Assess implementation readiness across ten systems. No response is transmitted, stored or used for lead capture. The score is not a Google ranking score and cannot predict visibility or appointments.

Technical eligibility

Can every intended indexable page be reached through a standard crawlable link?
Do canonical URLs, robots directives and sitemap inclusion agree?
Has mobile, keyboard, broken-link and performance QA passed?

Local entity truth

Is the clinic's approved name, address, phone and hours recorded from real evidence?
Are clinic, branch and practitioner relationships explicitly verified?
Are fake localities, virtual offices and keyword-added business names blocked?

GBP readiness

Is an authorized profile owner and rollback path recorded?
Are categories and services based on the actual operating model?
Does every proposed profile change have evidence and an approver?

Service-page evidence

Can the clinic prove each published service is offered at the named branch?
Are provider, process, limitation and CTA claims owner-approved?
Are outcome, pain, permanence and superiority shortcuts rejected?

Content quality

Does each page solve one distinct reader decision?
Does every article include an original tool, framework or decision aid?
Are agent assistance, accountable publisher and review limits visible?

AEO answer readiness

Do important questions receive a concise answer followed by evidence and limits?
Are answer units visible to people rather than hidden for crawlers?
Does each answer lead to the next useful page without diagnosing a patient?

GEO provenance

Are externally verifiable claims supported by nearby authoritative sources?
Do source dates, publisher identity and terminology remain consistent?
Does structured data match the visible page and avoid invented entities?

Reviews and reputation

Are feedback requests optional, neutral and free of incentives or gating?
Do public response rules protect patient privacy?

Authority and backlinks

Are owned, social, prospect, submission and editorial links classified separately?
Are paid links, mass directories, PBNs and reciprocal schemes blocked?

Measurement

Are eligibility, indexation, visibility, enquiries and revenue reported as separate layers?
Do every metric, date range, unavailable state and attribution limit have a clear definition?
