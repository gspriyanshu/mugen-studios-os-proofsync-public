Trust begins with named responsibility

A real clinic about page should identify accountable people only after their identity, registration, role and approval have been verified. A polished portrait or impressive claim is not a substitute for evidence.

✦

What a real profile should make clear

  1. Full professional name and role.
  2. Registration source and verification date.
  3. Qualifications and memberships supported by evidence.
  4. Who wrote, clinically reviewed and approved each page.
  5. When the information will be reviewed again.

Responsibility in this simulation

No practitioner is attached to this fictional interface. MUGEN Studios prepared the design and editorial structure; no clinical review has taken place.

How can you tell whether clinical information has responsible ownership?

Look for a named author, a qualified reviewer, verifiable professional details, a review date and a clear correction route.

Responsibility Real-clinic facts and a qualified reviewer would be required.

Limitation If those details are missing, treat the information as incomplete.
