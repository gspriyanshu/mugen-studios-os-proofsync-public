Know what to verify before a first visit

A first-visit page should answer practical questions before it asks for personal information. In a real clinic, every location, access, fee and policy detail would need evidence and an accountable owner.

What information should a clinic provide before a first visit?

It should provide verified identity and access details, clear fees and cancellation terms, an explanation of how information is handled, and the name of the person responsible for clinical content.

Responsibility Real-clinic facts and a qualified reviewer would be required.

Limitation This simulation has none of those real-clinic facts.
