A consultation should begin with questions

A responsible page should not presume a diagnosis or steer you towards a procedure. It should show how a real clinic would explain responsibility, evidence, alternatives, costs and consent before an individual decision.

✦

Questions worth bringing to the conversation

  1. Who is qualified and responsible for the assessment?
  2. What information would be needed before options could be discussed?
  3. How would alternatives, costs and consent be explained?
  4. Who would answer questions after the consultation?

What this page does not do

This simulation cannot answer those questions for a clinic that does not exist. It names no procedure, timeline or expected result.
