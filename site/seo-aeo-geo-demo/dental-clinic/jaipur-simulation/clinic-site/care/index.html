The page should state its limits

These pathways describe the information a responsible website should provide. They do not state that care is offered, determine whether any option is suitable or replace a clinical assessment.

What should a transparent care page explain?

It should explain the purpose of the conversation, who is responsible for the information, which facts still need individual assessment, and where practical policies can be found.

Responsibility Real-clinic facts and a qualified reviewer would be required.
