Your questions belong in the plan

Appearance-related information should support a careful conversation, not present one ideal result. A responsible clinic should explain who is accountable, what requires individual assessment and how images or personal information would be used.

✦

Keep the conversation grounded

  1. Ask who prepared and reviewed the information.
  2. Ask how alternatives and costs would be explained.
  3. Ask how expectations and limitations would be documented.
  4. Ask for the purpose and consent terms before any image is used.

No result theatre

This simulation contains no patient image, testimonial, result or appearance promise.
