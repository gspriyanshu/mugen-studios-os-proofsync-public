Jaipur dental website simulation · 14 July 2026

A Jaipur dental website built for patients first.

This capability study corrects a common portfolio mistake: a clinic website should explain care, visits, responsibility and access—not advertise the delivery methods behind it. The search, answer, entity, local and authority work is documented here, outside the patient experience.

Simulation boundary

The clinic interface is fictional and Jaipur is the simulation market. No clinic entity, Google Business Profile, practitioner, patient, address, contact channel or result is represented. Clinical content would require a real qualified dentist. Backlink prospects and drafts are not acquired editorial backlinks. Search eligibility does not prove indexation or rankings.

01 / The correction

One project, two honest public layers.

Patient layer

Ten distinct decisions, calm clinic language, no delivery-method jargon, no invented people or local facts, and no active contact flow. Every route is noindex,follow and absent from the sitemap.

Capability layer

This indexable study and checklist explain market research, page ownership, answer design, provenance, technical boundaries, local implementation, authority development and measurement.

03 / Keyword evidence

Demand informed architecture; it did not write claims.

In the fresh Jaipur Keyword Planner session, “dentist Jaipur” and “dental clinic Jaipur” showed 1K–10K average monthly searches; “pediatric dentist Jaipur,” “dental implants Jaipur” and “orthodontist Jaipur” showed 100–1K. “Dentist near me” and “dental clinic near me” also showed 1K–10K for the selected Jaipur market. Values are broad planning ranges for Jul 2025–Jun 2026, not traffic forecasts.

Important: Google Ads competition is advertising competition, not organic keyword difficulty. Emergency and root-canal inputs were removed by the interface from the idea set; other inputs without a visible provided-row metric were recorded honestly rather than filled by assumption.

06 / Local and authority system

Local readiness begins with evidence, not a fictional listing.

The real-client implementation template requires verified legal and public name, address evidence, telephone ownership, hours, categories, services, practitioner relationships, appointment URL, accessibility attributes, website/profile/schema parity, change approval, monitoring, rollback and privacy. Every field is marked REAL_CLINIC_DATA_REQUIRED.

The authority packet qualifies real editorial routes and prepares three target-specific drafts. Nothing was sent. No directory listing or citation was created for the fictional label. One repository-controlled link becomes a live owned link only after source, destination, anchor, relation, merge and deployment are verified.
