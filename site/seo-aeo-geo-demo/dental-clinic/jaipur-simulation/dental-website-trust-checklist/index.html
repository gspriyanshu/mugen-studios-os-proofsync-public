The dental website trust checklist.

Use this before publishing a clinic page or approving an agency deliverable. It separates verified identity, patient clarity, clinical responsibility, technical eligibility and authority quality.

Use boundary

This is a nonclinical portfolio resource, not patient guidance or a substitute for legal, regulatory or dental review. The companion clinic interface is fictional and Jaipur is the simulation market. No clinic entity, Google Business Profile, practitioner, patient or outcome is represented. Clinical content would require a real qualified dentist. Backlink prospects and drafts are not acquired editorial backlinks. Search eligibility does not prove indexation or rankings.

Twenty-five questions before contact becomes a call to action.

Mark an item complete only when the supporting evidence exists and has an accountable owner.

  1. Identity

    Does the page show the clinic’s verified public identity without invented location claims?

  2. Practitioner responsibility

    Can a visitor verify the responsible practitioner, registration source, role and review date?

  3. Clinical review

    Does every clinical explanation identify a qualified reviewer and the last review date?

  4. Care-page limits

    Does the page explain its purpose without diagnosing, assuming suitability or promising an outcome?

  5. First-visit clarity

    Are identity, access, policy and information-handling facts available before data is requested?

  6. Fees and cancellations

    Are applicable fees, payment terms and cancellation rules written in plain language and dated?

  7. Urgent limitations

    Does qualified wording make after-hours and urgent-support limits clear without unsafe triage?

  8. Accessibility

    Are access and communication features specific, current and supported by the real facility?

  9. Privacy

    Is the purpose, storage, sharing and owner of each requested field explained before collection?

  10. Contact accuracy

    Do website, public profile and structured facts agree on verified contact details and hours?

  11. Direct answers

    Can a visitor find a concise answer, its support, responsibility, limitation and safe next step together?

  12. Source quality

    Are claims supported by current official, professional or primary sources rather than copied summaries?

  13. Authorship

    Is it clear who drafted, reviewed, approved and will update the page?

  14. Image consent

    Are patient images used only with appropriate permission and truthful context?

  15. Reviews

    Are reviews genuine, unedited in meaning and free of prohibited incentives or invented rating markup?

  16. Schema parity

    Does structured data mirror visible truth and avoid fake clinic, person, review or action entities?

  17. Indexability

    Are real public pages eligible while prototypes, drafts and fictional entity pages remain excluded?

  18. Canonicals

    Does every public route declare the exact preferred URL?

  19. Internal paths

    Can visitors and crawlers reach each important page through descriptive, varied links?

  20. Mobile use

    Can a visitor read, navigate and focus every control at a small viewport without horizontal overflow?

  21. Performance

    Does the page stay useful with lightweight assets, stable layout and minimal interaction cost?

  22. Local evidence

    Are address, telephone, hours, categories and practitioner relationships backed by real-client proof?

  23. Authority

    Are links earned through useful editorial assets rather than payment, automation, fake profiles or mass exchange?

  24. Measurement

    Are eligibility, discovery, visibility, engagement and outcomes reported as separate states?

  25. Corrections

    Is there an accountable route for correcting outdated patient or clinic information?
