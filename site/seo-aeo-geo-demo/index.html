It demonstrates implementation readiness—not rankings, traffic, leads or AI citations.

Truth boundary: NESTRA is speculative portfolio work. The routes are index-eligible, internally connected, and sitemap-listed; external discovery and indexation remain pending. One foundation, three responsibilities

Search visibility without the theatre.

SEO

Make useful content technically eligible, aligned to intent, internally connected, fast enough to use, and measurable after release.

AEO

Give real questions concise, accurate answers with enough supporting facts, decisions, limitations, and expert context to remain useful.

GEO

Clarify entities, relationships, provenance, original evidence, and source paths so generative systems can retrieve and attribute information responsibly.

Fixed internal evaluator

Three cycles, one unchanged scoring contract.

The source and every candidate were judged against the same 100-point internal artifact rubric. These numbers measure completeness and safety of the work package; they are not market-performance scores.

Method

Baseline → candidate → implementation → validation → decision.

Observe

Freeze the source, evidence boundaries, evaluator, target route, risks, and rollback before changing anything.

Build and test

Map requirements to pages, implement the smallest useful change, then test content, schema, links, metadata, privacy, and performance.

Keep or discard

Retain only a measurable artifact improvement with no critical regression. A score never grants promotion or proves an outcome.
