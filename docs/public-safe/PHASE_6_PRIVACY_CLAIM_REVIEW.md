# Phase 6 Privacy And Claim Review

Review status: prepared public-safe summary review.

The Phase 6 summary files are title-level architecture summaries. They do not copy private source documents wholesale and do not include local paths, private evidence, client data, connector results, account identifiers, Drive identifiers, website source, tracking IDs, or raw assets.

## Claim Controls

The summaries do not claim:

- service readiness
- client readiness
- deployed website status
- live testing completion
- demo asset completion
- connector execution
- Drive upload
- public release approval

## Phase 6 Boundary

Phase 6 unlocks only public-safe summaries. Phase 7 remains locked until the Phase 6 end audit passes.
