# V2.12 Storage And Evidence Summary

Large or sensitive assets do not belong in the public repository.

Private storage can later hold screenshots, videos, render outputs, reports, raw exports, and review material when a phase gate approves that storage route. Public records may point to reviewed summaries or placeholders, but must not expose private storage identifiers.

## Public-Safe Storage Rule

Public files may contain:

- placeholder pointers
- policy descriptions
- retention rules
- privacy and rights gates
- compact evidence-capsule concepts

Public files must not contain:

- real Drive identifiers
- local machine paths
- raw screenshots
- raw videos
- failed renders
- private exports
- client material
- unreviewed generated assets
