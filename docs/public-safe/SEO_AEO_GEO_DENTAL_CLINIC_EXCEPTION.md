# Dental-clinic SEO/AEO/GEO portfolio exception

Date: 2026-07-14

## Approved purpose

This exception permits a public-safe dental-sector capability demonstration below:

`/seo-aeo-geo-demo/dental-clinic/`

The release is a MUGEN Studios OS portfolio system for dental SEO, local search, AEO, GEO, content governance, authority and measurement. It is not a dental clinic or patient-service website.

## Exact scope

The allowed scope contains:

- A portfolio landing page.
- Service and methodology pages.
- A sixteen-article owner-facing field library comprising four pillars and twelve supports.
- A twenty-seven-question, browser-only readiness scorecard with no storage, transmission or lead capture.
- One fictional clinic-interface preview using `noindex,follow` and excluded from the sitemap.
- One lightweight local stylesheet and one scorecard script without network, cookie or browser-storage access.

No city, neighbourhood, procedure, practitioner, patient, appointment or emergency route is approved.

## Entity and healthcare boundary

The public pages must not contain or encode a fictional address, telephone, opening hours, coordinates, practitioner, credential, patient story, review, rating, service availability or treatment outcome.

`Dentist`, `MedicalBusiness`, `LocalBusiness`, `Person`, `Review`, `AggregateRating`, `Offer` and appointment-action markup remain forbidden. The fictional preview is never listed in the sitemap.

The sixteen indexable articles address practice owners, managers and marketing teams. They do not provide diagnosis, symptom triage, individual treatment selection, medication, emergency, recovery, cost, insurance or outcome advice. Patient-health publication requires a later real-clinic engagement and qualified clinical review.

## Authority and performance boundary

The repository README link is an owned external-domain link after successful protected merge, Pages deployment and live verification. It is never an acquired editorial backlink.

Prospects and unsent drafts remain separate private evidence. Rankings, Local Pack visibility, traffic, enquiries, patient outcomes, editorial backlinks and AI citations remain pending unless independently observed later.

## Release controls

The change must use a feature branch, pull request, required validation, protected merge, Pages deployment and live route verification. Existing protected root, verification and deployment assets remain unchanged.

Public account screenshots, customer identifiers, credentials, cookies, private prompts and raw research exports are not allowed in the repository.
