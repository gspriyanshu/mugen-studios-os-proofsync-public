# V2.12 Composite Service Lifecycle Summary

The website and public repository are designed to support composite services across a full lifecycle, not only final demonstrations.

Lifecycle support includes:

- planning a service requirement
- deciding whether a website is needed
- preparing a safe test route
- validating connector and platform needs
- recording quality gates
- producing private evidence
- selecting derivative demonstration material after approval
- reusing safe patterns across industries and scenarios

## Stage Discipline

Stage 2 live testing is not automatic. A service must first pass a questioning gate that decides whether a website, connector, report, screenshot, video, or other environment is actually required.

Stage 3 demonstration material requires Stage 2 lineage and approval. Public-facing material must be derivative, reviewed, and claim-controlled.
